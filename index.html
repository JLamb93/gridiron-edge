How these projections are built +

Every number on this page is computed in your browser from nfl_data.json, a file built by a script (data-pipeline/build_data.py) that pulls real public data: every game log, pass-direction split, and sack/pressure count comes straight from nflverse's play-by-play archive (the same data NFL broadcasts are built on); records, streaks, home/away splits, and the week's schedule come from ESPN's public standings and scoreboard endpoints. Nothing here depends on a paid odds feed or a hand-typed number — re-running the script and republishing that one JSON file refreshes the entire site. Lines marked ~ are this season's pace rather than a live sportsbook quote, since no source here provides betting lines.

1. Blended baseline

50% last-3-game average, 30% full-season average, 20% most recent game — recent form counts, but one outlier game can't swing it.

2. Matchup adjustment

Shifts the baseline up to ±12% based on the opponent's rank against that position — a bottom-5 defense nudges the projection up, a top-5 defense pulls it down.

3. Availability discount

Questionable/probable tags shave the projection (7% / 2%) for expected snap or target share loss. A player ruled Out is flagged and skipped.

4. Probability, not certainty

Game-to-game variance (from a real log, or a generic estimate when one isn't on file) sets a spread around the projection; that spread estimates the odds of clearing the line — shown as a percentage, never a lock.

5. Every QB's field chart is real

Next Gen Stats' free pages don't publish a per-player field-side/depth breakdown, but nflverse's raw play-by-play logs the actual target location and air yards on every attempt — so each starting QB's passing-tendency field here is their own measured shape, not a league-average guess.

6. Roster coverage

Every team's leading passer, plus its top 2 rushers, top 3 receivers, and top tight end by volume, auto-qualify once they clear a minimum-attempts floor — no hand-picking, so the roster updates itself as workloads shift week to week.

This is an analytical estimate, not a guarantee. Projections are built from a transparent statistical blend of historical performance and matchup context — they do not account for in-game events, weather, or late-breaking news beyond what's shown, and they are not financial or betting advice.
